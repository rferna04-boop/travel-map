Field Notes

Azores Expedition Journal

Positioned 1,400 kilometers west of Lisbon in the middle of the North Atlantic Ocean, the Azores archipelago is characterized by dramatic stratovolcanoes, thermal steam fissures, and lush endemism nurtured by continuous ocean mist.

This photographic archive focuses primarily on São Miguel—the largest island—documenting the twin crater lakes of Sete Cidades, the boiling geothermal springs of Furnas, and the rugged Atlantic cliffheads where basalt black lava meets the ocean swell.

Equipment: Fujifilm X-T5, XF 23mm f/2 R WR, XF 33mm f/1.4 R LM WR, and iPhone 15 Pro.

Studio Profile

About the Project

FERNANDES is an editorial photography and visual design studio exploring topography, geography, and architecture.
